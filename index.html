Products I build and run myself

Almanac

Household finance · used by a household of two · agents in development

Almanac already turns bank statements into a reconciled ledger. Categorisation weighs several signals, from merchant matching and web enrichment to Claude batch classification, and a two-framing consensus sends every disagreement to a person.

Next, it becomes a multi-agent system that runs our household finances. Each agent gets only the tools it needs, served over MCP, and nothing is written without a person confirming it.

Analyst
Answers from the household ledger through 15 read-only tools.
Forecaster
Projects income and spending from the monthly series.
Planner
Turns a goal, like a house deposit, into a savings plan and tracks it.
Researcher
Checks live mortgage rates on the web.
